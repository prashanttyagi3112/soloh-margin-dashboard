Client Health, Methods and Fees

The CFO's method, and the MSP fee and NBO hours used for each client.

Client Health = 70% start rate + 30% extension rate. Start rate = (started + ¾ × onboarding) ÷ (started + onboarding + cancelled offers), counted per candidate's first contract. Extension rate = share of finished first contracts that extended (left out until one has finished). 75%+ healthy · 50–74% watch · under 50% at risk. Baptist and Baptist Winter Reduced MSP count as one client; so do both NYC H+H rows.
